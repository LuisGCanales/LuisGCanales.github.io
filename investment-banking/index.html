Selected professional engagement · International healthcare research consulting

Reframing a Client Problem into an Implemented Analytical Solution

A clinical research team initially requested the adoption of an established statistical methodology. After reviewing the literature and their research objectives, I identified important limitations in transferring that approach to their data and intended questions.

Through successive discussions, I clarified the team’s objectives, challenged the original assumptions and designed an alternative probabilistic framework. With stakeholder agreement, I implemented and validated it using their clinical data, producing estimates, analytical reports, presentations and revisions to the research manuscript.

The work provided a more appropriate analytical foundation for understanding patient outcomes and potential future evidence-based decisions. Throughout the engagement, I explained the rationale, findings, limitations and alternatives to stakeholders without specialized statistical training.

03 / Understanding the function

Analysis in service of the client’s decision.

What attracts me to Investment Banking is the connection between understanding a business, evaluating financial alternatives and helping develop a solution that can be executed. This is how I understand the workflow I want to learn within an experienced team.

The analyst’s contribution includes detailed research, updating models, preparing materials, checking figures, incorporating feedback and keeping deliverables consistent under deadlines. I want to learn that daily work and its standards.

  1. Understand the objective.

    Clarify the client’s business, priorities, constraints and decision context.

  2. Build the financial picture.

    Analyze performance, cash generation, capital structure, the industry and relevant market conditions.

  3. Evaluate alternatives.

    Develop and test assumptions, valuation approaches and scenarios for strategic or financing decisions.

  4. Assess execution requirements.

    Consider transaction economics, feasibility, due diligence, documentation and coordination with legal, regulatory and other specialists.

  5. Support communication and execution.

    Prepare and refine materials, respond to review and support the solution through the institutional process.
