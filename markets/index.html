From an exposure to a decision

In a structuring or trading team, I want to support the work behind those decisions by following markets, checking pricing inputs and preparing analysis that colleagues can review and act on.

  1. Start with the exposure

    Identify what the client needs to protect or achieve, the relevant cash flows and maturities, and the market conditions affecting the decision.

  2. Compare products and scenarios

    Assess the economics of feasible alternatives, reprice them under plausible scenarios and identify the sensitivities and exposures that remain.

  3. Prepare for execution

    Check quotes, conventions and liquidity. Summarise the economics, flag execution constraints and prepare materials for review and documentation.

  4. Monitor and validate

    Track market moves and exposures, investigate discrepancies, and document what changed and when it needs review.

Financial implementation

MXN interest-rate curves

I built an F-TIIE OIS curve from 15 synthetic swap quotes, implemented coupon and cash-flow conventions, and verified the fit with a separate instrument repricing pass.

Three methods fit the quotes almost identically, yet they implied different forward rates between calibration pillars. The study shows how that choice can change projected floating payments and the way quote shocks propagate across maturities.
